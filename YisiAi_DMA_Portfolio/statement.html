Artist Statement

My work explores how systems—digital, social, and environmental—shape how we communicate and behave. I am interested in the tension between structured rules and individual freedom.

In my digital projects, I build interactive environments that simulate how platforms influence us. Projects such as PoliteChat and a speculative online marketplace explore how technology can reshape language, ethics, and social interaction. These works encourage viewers to notice how system design can guide emotions, decisions, and everyday behavior.

I also work with photography and sculpture to observe similar ideas in real life. By documenting urban spaces and moments of youth and responsibility, I explore how personal identity develops within larger social environments. Across these different media, my work invites viewers to pause and reflect on how the systems around us influence daily experience.
