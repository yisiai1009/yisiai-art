Work Artist Statement

The Weight of Sin

Sculpture · Welded Metal

The Weight of Sin is a sculpture made from welded wire and metal rods. The figure resembles a small demon with wings, holding a heavy steel rose. The rose represents temptation and desire, while the demon’s painful posture suggests the burden that desire can create. The work draws from the symbolism of the Seven Deadly Sins and reflects on how attraction and struggle can exist at the same time.
