Cat’s Day

Interactive Web Project · 2025

Click here to be a cat →

Cat’s Day is an interactive web project about experiencing a day like a cat.

Instead of following a fixed schedule, users can choose actions freely, such as eating, playing, patrolling, or sleeping. There is no timeline or required order.

The project shows how animals experience time in a more flexible way. It also invites viewers to think about their own daily routines and how schedules shape the way people live.
